Status
Implemented and locally validated in the existing security worktree. Not committed,

Behaviour
- Online quote submission records the specific quote/mobile request, source,
  timestamp, actor and notice version 2026-10-06. The final form step shows the
  notification notice and an email-only choice. Old forms without the current
  notice version can still obtain an email but do not gain SMS permission.
- The shared owner/regional-agent final-quote composer has an unchecked
  confirmation that the client requested the quote, supplied the mobile for quote
  contact, and was told about the SMS and opt-out. Selecting it records the request
  at send time, using the authenticated actor and reviewed document's mobile.
  Existing online request evidence works without another staff confirmation.
- Evidence applies only to that quote/mobile. It is not global marketing consent.
  Original request evidence is retained on retries. An online email-only choice
  cannot be overridden by the agent confirmation. Existing global STOP/opt-outs
  remain authoritative at dispatch.
- One fixed factual notification follows provider acceptance of an online estimate;
  one follows each emailed final quote version. Resends do not add notifications.
  Online estimates use a separate provider-acceptance receipt and version 0.
  Final publication cancels any pending estimate notification it supersedes.
- The existing worker, provider idempotency keys, receipt reconciliation, sender
  checks, credit controls, expiry, regional authorization and no-live-send guard
  are retained. Inferred request permission cannot authorise arbitrary manual SMS.
- Zero delay means the next scheduler run during Mon-Fri 9am-6pm in the service
  region, normally within a minute; after-hours/weekends wait until next weekday
  9am. Existing five-minute default changes to zero in the forward migration.
  Other configured delays are retained. Prior queued dates are not rewritten.
- Estimate/final notices for the same quote are spaced at least one minute apart.
  Daily recipient cooldown remains across unrelated quotes; unresolved provider
  sends still block another submission. Suppression and expiry can prevent delivery.
- Terms, privacy page, public form and remote/final quote email footers explain
  SMS purpose, scope, STOP/email-only and record handling. Contact email unchanged.

Source review
ACMA guidance distinguishes context-supported inferred consent from simply having
contact details, and requires honouring withdrawals:
https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam
The feature therefore limits inferred permission to a requested quote notification
and records the request context rather than creating blanket marketing consent.
Supabase changelog and current database function/security guidance were reviewed:
https://supabase.com/changelog.md
https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/functions
The reviewed recent extension/operator changes do not affect the built-in JSON,
row-locking, timestamp and RPC constructs introduced here.
